--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 IMPORTANT CORRECTION BEFORE YOU START
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your original demo notes said the fraud detection risk score would calculate to
"59.5". That figure is wrong. With the weights in the framework document:

    Risk = 0.30 x Impact + 0.20 x Likelihood + 0.15 x Privacy
         + 0.15 x Fairness + 0.10 x Security + 0.10 x Autonomy

    Impact 70    ->  0.30 x 70 = 21.00
    Likelihood 40->  0.20 x 40 =  8.00
    Privacy 80   ->  0.15 x 80 = 12.00
    Fairness 30  ->  0.15 x 30 =  4.50
    Security 60  ->  0.10 x 60 =  6.00
    Autonomy 50  ->  0.10 x 50 =  5.00
                                 ------
                                  56.50

The correct answer is **56.5**, which is still in the HIGH band (41-70), so your
narrative is unchanged - only the number. The platform computes this
automatically; an examiner checking the arithmetic will get 56.5. Do not say
59.5, or you invite a question you cannot answer.

Verified: `tests/functional_test.py` asserts this exact calculation.

 SECTION 0 - THE STORY YOU ARE TELLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Nigeria now has a Data Protection Act (2023) and a National AI Strategy. Nigerian
startups are deploying AI faster than they are governing it. The gap this project
fills is simple:

    A startup cannot govern an AI system it has not recorded, cannot judge one
    it has not assessed, and cannot prove any of it without a trail.

The platform delivers five things:

    1. An AI REGISTER        - every system recorded, owned and classified
    2. A RISK ENGINE         - six weighted dimensions, score calculated
    3. AN ETHICS ASSESSMENT  - eight requirements, recommendation produced
    4. AN APPROVAL WORKFLOW  - separation of duties, decisions recorded
    5. AN AUDIT TRAIL        - append-only evidence for the regulator

AND IT ENFORCES SEPARATION OF DUTIES: the developer who builds an AI system
cannot approve it. Only the Governance Officer decides, and High/Critical risk
systems need an Administrator.

The organisation in the demo data is **PayLink Nigeria Ltd**, a fictional
Nigerian fintech with three AI systems:

    Customer Support Chatbot        17.5  LOW       Approved
    Loan Approval Algorithm         63.8  HIGH      Under review
    Employee Performance Analyzer   (not assessed)  Submitted

        Security Exposure              60
        Autonomy / Decision Impact     50

   SAY: "The risk score is not a judgement, it is arithmetic. Six dimensions,
         each weighted."

   WHEN THE SCORE SHOWS 56.5:

   SAY: "56.5 out of 100. That places it in the HIGH risk band, which is 41 to 70.

         Let me show you why. Impact carries the heaviest weight at 0.30, because
         governance is ultimately about the severity of harm to people. Privacy
         and fairness are weighted equally at 0.15 each, because those are
         precisely the two harms the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and our
         anti-discrimination obligations focus on. Security and autonomy carry
         0.10 each - they modify exposure rather than create harm directly.

         The weights sum to exactly 1.00, so the score stays on the same 0-100
         scale as its inputs and is directly comparable between systems.

         And here is the breakdown: it tells me that impact is contributing 21
         of the 56.5 points and privacy 12. A governance officer can see not just
         the score, but WHY."

   CLICK  💾 Save this risk assessment

 SECTION 5 - QUESTIONS YOU WILL BE ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: "Why does the developer not approve their own system?"
A: "That is separation of duties, the central control in any governance
    framework. A developer is measured on shipping, so they have a structural
    incentive to understate risk. The person who benefits from a system being
    approved must not be the person who approves it. In the platform this is
    enforced technically through the permission matrix - the Developer role
    simply does not hold the 'decide_system' permission - so it cannot be
    bypassed by good intentions."

Q: "Where do the risk weights come from? Are they not arbitrary?"
A: "They are a policy choice, and I have made the rationale explicit.
    Impact carries 0.30 because governance is ultimately about severity of harm
    to people. Likelihood carries 0.20 - a severe but impossible failure is less
    urgent than a frequent moderate one. Privacy and fairness carry 0.15 each
    because those are the two harms the NDPA 2023 and our anti-discrimination
    obligations focus on. Security and autonomy carry 0.10 each because they
    modify exposure rather than create harm directly. The weights sum to exactly
    1.00, so the score stays on the same scale as its inputs. Importantly, they
    are declared as data in one module, so an organisation can change the policy
    in one place and every calculation follows."

Q: "Is a weighted average of six numbers not a false precision?"
A: "It is a structured judgement, not a measurement, and I say so in the
    interface. The value is not the decimal - it is that every system is judged
    on the same six dimensions, with the same weights, and the result is
    reproducible and auditable. The banding (Low/Moderate/High/Critical) is what
    drives the workflow. And the breakdown is shown alongside so the officer sees
    which dimensions drove the score rather than trusting a single number."

Q: "Why is 56.5 High and not Critical?"
A: "The bands are 0-20 Low, 21-40 Moderate, 41-70 High, 71-100 Critical.
    56.5 sits in the High band. Critical is reserved for systems that should be
    halted pending remediation."

Q: "What stops someone from editing the audit trail?"
A: "There is no code path that can update or delete an audit entry. The
    audit_logger module exposes only a write function and read functions - no
    update, no delete. That is a deliberate design constraint rather than a
    permission check, because a permission can be granted and a missing function
    cannot."

Q: "How does this align with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023?"
A: "Three direct mappings. First, the privacy question in the ethical assessment
    asks whether personal data is minimised, purpose-limited and protected.
    Second, the incident module is designed for NDPA breach notification - the
    incident report carries a regulatory note about notifying the Nigeria Data
    Protection Commission. Third, the accountability requirement forces a named
    responsible person for every AI system, which is what makes a data controller
    able to answer for its processing."

Q: "Why SQLite? Would a real deployment not need something bigger?"
A: "SQLite was chosen because it is built into Python, needs no server and no
    administration, and is fully ACID-compliant with real foreign keys and CHECK
    constraints - which I demonstrate. For a single startup that is the right
    engineering trade-off. All database access is isolated in one module, db.py,
    so migrating to PostgreSQL for a multi-tenant SaaS deployment means changing
    one file. I also run it in WAL mode with a busy timeout and retries, so
    concurrent sessions do not lock each other out - I tested that with
    simultaneous writers."

Q: "What is the difference between the risk assessment and the ethical
   assessment? Are they not the same thing?"
A: "No, and the distinction matters. Risk tells you how much harm is possible.
    Ethics tells you whether the system is being built and operated responsibly
    regardless of the probability - fairness, privacy, transparency,
    accountability, human oversight, security, explainability and accessibility.
    A system can be low risk and still unethical: a low-impact system that is
    completely unexplainable and has no accountable owner fails the ethical
    test even though the harm is small."

Q: "How did you test it?"
A: "Two automated suites. tests/functional_test.py checks the risk formula
    against twelve worked cases including all six single-dimension cases, the
    band boundaries at 20/40/70, the ethical recommendation rules, password
    hashing, all five roles' permissions, the full governance workflow, the CHECK
    and foreign key constraints, the audit trail, all three report generators and
    concurrent writes. tests/ui_smoke_test.py drives the real interface through
    Streamlit's test harness, proves each of the five roles sees exactly the
    pages its permissions allow and nothing more, and performs the entire
    defence-day workflow - register, assess, return, report, audit. Both suites
    pass with zero failures."

Q: "What would you do next?"
A: "A regulator-facing export aligned to a standard schema so startups can submit
    evidence without reformatting; continuous monitoring that re-scores the risk
    band when the data sources change; integration with the NDPA breach
    notification process; and a multi-tenant version so one platform can serve
    many startups with data isolation between them."

 SECTION 6 - IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 DATA LOOKS MESSY AFTER EXPERIMENTS
   > setup_and_start.bat reset   (rebuilds and re-seeds in seconds)
   > or: sign in as Administrator -> ⚙️ Settings -> Database health ->
     "Rebuild demonstration data"

 BROWSER TAB BLANK OR DISCONNECTED
   > Press F5. Streamlit reconnects and the session is preserved.

 SERVER WINDOW CLOSED
   > Run setup_and_start.bat again (about 20 seconds).

 PORT 8501 ALREADY IN USE
   > The launcher switches to 8502 automatically and opens the right address.

 CHARTS DO NOT RENDER
   > Click another menu item and come back. Plotly needs a moment on first load.

 YOU FORGET WHICH ACCOUNT YOU ARE IN
   > The sidebar always shows the signed-in name and role badge.

 A RED ERROR BOX APPEARS
   > It is caught by the error handler, not a crash. Note it, click another menu
     item, continue. Never debug in front of the panel.

 REHEARSE THE RECOVERY ONCE. Confidence in the recovery is worth more than
 perfection in the first run.
